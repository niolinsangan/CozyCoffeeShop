About Us

Café Story

CozyCup Café was born from a shared passion for exceptional coffee and genuine hospitality. As college friends turned business partners, Antonio Linsangan IV and Espencer Badilla dreamed of creating more than just another coffee shop – they envisioned a warm, welcoming space where every customer feels at home.

Café's Mission

Our mission is to provide a cozy and welcoming environment for coffee enthusiasts. We believe that great coffee has the power to bring people together, and we strive to make every visit memorable with our high-quality coffee and friendly service.
